Why Shopify?

Shopify holds a massive share of the global eCommerce ecosystem. Sourcing, theme development, and coding present unlimited remote career growth.

Local Sourcing & Dropshipping

Unlike international dropshipping, the Indian market has unique components like COD (Cash on Delivery) and logistics providers (Shiprocket, Delhivery) that require specialized setups to scale profitably.

Custom Theme Coding

Learn Shopify Liquid coding, theme architectures, template logic, configuration panels (settings_schema.json), dynamic custom sections, and push code directly through Shopify CLI.
